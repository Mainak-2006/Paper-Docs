Privacy Policy

Paper Docs — an offline-first document editor for Android.

Effective 19 October 2026

Summary

Your documents live on your device. Paper Docs only sends anything to the cloud if you choose to sign in, and when it does, it uses your own private storage under your own Google account. There is no advertising, no analytics inside the app, and no selling of data.

What stays on your device

Paper Docs keeps everything in a private database inside the app's own storage:

  • Your documents, including their text and formatting
  • Up to 30 earlier versions of each document, for recovery
  • Private notes you attach to a document
  • Your starred, trashed, and sort preferences

None of this is transmitted unless you sign in and sync.

What is sent to Google, and only if you sign in

Your account Signing in with Google shares your Google account identifier, display name, email address, and profile photo URL with Paper Docs, through Firebase Authentication.
Your documents When sync is on, Paper Docs uploads each document's title, its full text, and its formatting to your private Firestore database, filed under your account so that only you (and anyone you explicitly share with) can read it.
Being online in a shared document While you have a document open, Paper Docs records that you are viewing it — your account identifier, name, and photo — so that other people working in the same document can see that you are there.
Finding collaborators by email So that another person can share a document with you by typing your email address, Paper Docs publishes a lookup entry that maps your email address to your account identifier. Any signed-in Paper Docs user can read that lookup, which means a signed-in user could discover which email addresses have an account. It contains no other information. A signed-in user can only publish an entry for their own verified address, so nobody can register your address in the lookup or receive documents shared with it.

Google Drive

Drive access is optional and is requested only when you choose it. Paper Docs requests two scopes:

  • drive.file — create and manage only the files Paper Docs itself creates.
  • drive.readonly — list and read those same files back when you import them.

Paper Docs does not read, list, or modify files in your Drive that it did not create, and it never searches your Drive's contents. Copies you export are stored in your Drive in the location you pick.

Sharing a document

If you share a document, the person you name can open it. You choose whether they are a Viewer (read and export only) or an Editor (can also change the text). You can remove someone's access at any time; the app then stops syncing that document for them.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising networks.
  • No usage analytics or profiling inside the app.
  • No crash reporting service.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No collection of your location, contacts, photos, or files outside the app.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Internet — to sync your documents and to reach Google Drive.
  • Network state — to detect when you are offline and defer syncing.

Paper Docs asks for no location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permission.

Your choices and control

  • Stay offline. Everything works with no account. Nothing is transmitted.
  • Sign out. Removes the account link from the device. Your documents remain on the device and are not deleted.
  • Revoke Drive. You can remove Paper Docs' Drive access at any time from your Google account settings.
  • Delete a document. Permanently deleting a document removes the cloud copy, the document's version history, and the local copy.
  • Delete everything from the cloud. Account settings include Delete my cloud data, which permanently removes every document Paper Docs has uploaded for your account, plus your presence entries, your shared-document listings, and the email lookup entry described above. Your documents remain on this device.
  • Delete your account. Account settings also include Delete my account and data. This removes your documents, versions, and notes from both this device and the cloud, removes your presence and sharing entries from other people's documents, and deletes your Paper Docs account from Firebase Authentication. Your Google account itself is not affected, and you can create a new Paper Docs account at any time by signing in again. This cannot be undone.

Deleting a document removes it everywhere it is stored, so it cannot be recovered from the cloud afterwards. Cloud deletions require an internet connection; if one cannot be completed the app tells you and does not delete the local copy, so nothing is left in an inconsistent state.

Children

Paper Docs is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Documents are stored in a private database that is readable only by you and the people you share with. Access is enforced by Firestore security rules on Google's servers, not merely by the app.
