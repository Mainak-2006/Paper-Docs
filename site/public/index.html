Paper Docs

Offline-first documents for Android.

A native rich-text editor that keeps your words on your device, and syncs them only when you ask it to.

Read the privacy policy

What it does

Offline first

Documents, version history and notes live in the app. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you sign in.

Sync & sharing

Sync documents to your own private cloud storage, and share them as a viewer or editor.

Real formatting

Headings, lists, tables, links, colour and alignment — exported to PDF, Word, HTML and plain text.

Drive backup

Export a copy to Google Drive, or import one back, limited to files Paper Docs created.

Privacy in one line

No advertising, no analytics, no data selling. If you never sign in, nothing leaves your device. See the full policy for exactly what is stored where.
